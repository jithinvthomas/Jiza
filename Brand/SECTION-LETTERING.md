Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using `jiza-wordmark-flat-v1.png` as the style reference. Files: `jiza-lettering-{music,video,browser,download,trading}-v1.png`. Original transparent outputs are preserved; the preview uses their alpha masks for theme colors.

Prompt set: Generate exactly Music, Video, Browser, Download, and Trading, one asset per word, capital initial and lowercase remaining letters. Match Jiza's upright vertical stems, substantial uniform strokes, softly rounded geometric terminals, tight optical spacing, circular counters, single-storey a/g, and slanted rounded-lozenge i dots. Flat charcoal on genuine transparency, no italic slant, gradients, shadows, panels, icons, or added Jiza text. Crisp edges and small-header legibility.

These are companion lettering concepts, not an exact font or installable typeface. Integrated into the live preview for review; native asset integration is pending.
